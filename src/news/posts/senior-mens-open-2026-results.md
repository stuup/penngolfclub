Our final open of the 2026 season, the Senior Men's Open Am/Am, was played on Thursday 20th August and produced a finish to remember: the top three teams were separated by just one point.

**Winners — 88 points:** P. Hill, S. Banks, G. Arnold & M. Sheffield (Penn)

**2nd — 87 points:** A. Duffield, M. Redding, K. Gelder & S. Picken (Penn)

**3rd — 87 points:** D. Gillespie, S. Mills, N. Mills & C. Harrison (Bloxwich)

Thank you to every team who joined us, and to the greenkeeping, catering and shop teams who made the day such a success. We look forward to seeing you all again next season.
