The Men's Open for the Howard Thirlby Rosebowl was played as an individual medal on Saturday 25th July 2026.

In Division 1 (plus to 6), **Kieran Turner** took best gross honours with a 75 (68 nett), with **Karl Philip Young** claiming first nett on 72.

In Division 2 (7 to 11), **Jason Walters** won on countback from **Si Bevan**, both returning nett 67.

Congratulations to all our winners, and thank you to everyone who entered.
