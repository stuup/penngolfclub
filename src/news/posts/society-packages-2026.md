Our summer society packages run from 1st April to 31st October 2026. Every package includes 18 holes on our heathland course and a warm welcome in the clubhouse, with food available all day.

Choose from **Starter** (bacon sandwich, tea or coffee and 18 holes) at £51.75, all the way up to **Platinum** with a three-course meal at £75.75 on weekdays. Lunch can be added to any package from £9.

Our in-house society organiser will look after every detail, and we can help you run competitions such as longest drive and nearest the pin.
