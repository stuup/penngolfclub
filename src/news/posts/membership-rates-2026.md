Our membership rates from 1st April 2026 are now published. Seven-day membership costs £1,452, which is less than £28 a week for unrestricted golf on one of the finest heathland courses in the Midlands.

- **Seven Day** — £1,452
- **Restricted** (weekdays plus Sunday afternoons) — £1,162
- **Five Day** — £1,089
- **Veteran** (75 and over) — £726
- **Juniors** — from just £15

We also offer a competitive joining rate for golfers aged 18–32, and a **two-for-one offer for ladies**: join with a friend or family member. Tuesday is Ladies' Day, so why not come and meet the section for a coffee?
